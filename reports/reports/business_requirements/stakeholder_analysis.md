# Stakeholder Analysis

## Purpose

This analysis identifies the stakeholders who will use the consumer shopping analysis, the decisions they need to make, and the KPIs that support those decisions.

| Stakeholder | Business Need | Key Questions | Relevant KPIs | Deliverable |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Senior Management | Overall business performance | Where are the main growth opportunities? | Total recorded purchase value, average purchase value, category contribution | Executive dashboard |
| Marketing Manager | Customer targeting | Which customer groups should receive targeted promotions? | Purchase value by demographic, discount usage rate, subscription rate | Customer analysis |
| Sales Manager | Sales performance | Which products, locations and seasons perform best? | Purchase value by item, category, location and season | Sales dashboard |
| Product Manager | Product and merchandising decisions | Which products perform well or receive poor ratings? | Product purchase count, value and average rating | Product analysis |
| Customer Experience Team | Customer satisfaction | Which products or shipping types receive lower ratings? | Average rating, low-rating rate, rating by shipping type | Satisfaction report |
| Finance Team | Promotion evaluation | Are discounted purchases associated with higher purchase values? | Discount usage rate, discounted value contribution, average purchase-value difference | Discount analysis |
| Operations Team | Shipping planning | Which shipping methods are most frequently selected? | Shipping-method usage and purchase value by shipping type | Operations dashboard |

## Stakeholder Prioritisation

| Stakeholder | Influence | Interest | Engagement Approach |
|---|---|---|---|
| Senior Management | High | High | Manage closely |
| Marketing Manager | High | High | Manage closely |
| Sales Manager | High | High | Manage closely |
| Product Manager | Medium | High | Keep actively involved |
| Finance Team | High | Medium | Keep satisfied |
| Customer Experience Team | Medium | High | Keep informed |
| Operations Team | Medium | Medium | Provide relevant updates |

## Important Limitation

The dataset does not contain dates, costs, profit margins, campaign responses or repeated customer transactions. Therefore, some stakeholder requirements—such as profit, retention, churn and campaign ROI—cannot be measured from the available data.
